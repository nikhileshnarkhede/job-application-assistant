### Part 2: Rubric Scoring

Score each category from 1-4:

**Format (Weight: 20%)**
- 4: Fills page, no errors, easy to scan
- 3: Almost fills page, minor issues
- 2: Font/spacing not appealing, errors present
- 1: Too short/long, multiple errors

**Education (Weight: 20%)**
- 4: Complete, organized, includes all relevant info
- 3: Well organized but missing some extras
- 2: Basic info only, not well organized
- 1: Missing crucial information

**Experience (Weight: 40%)**
- 4: Well-defined, action verbs, quantified achievements
- 3: Good but not detailed enough
- 2: Paragraph form, missing details
- 1: No order, incomplete information

**Activities/Honors (Weight: 20%)**
- 4: Well organized with skills and leadership
- 3: Includes info but difficult to follow
- 2: Missing key information
- 1: Missing or very little info
