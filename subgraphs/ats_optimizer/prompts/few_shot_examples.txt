## KEY PRINCIPLES FROM THESE EXAMPLES

1. **Bundle related keywords** - "algorithms" + "data structures" go together naturally
2. **Always add metrics** - Numbers make keywords more impactful AND more believable
3. **Fix format issues simultaneously** - Remove pronouns, shorten bullets while adding keywords
4. **Use exact JD phrasing** - If JD says "investigating and designing", use those exact words
5. **Skills section is high-value** - Direct additions there are simple and effective
6. **Action verbs matter** - "Architected", "Engineered", "Led" are stronger than "Built", "Made", "Did"
7. **Show scale** - "50TB", "100-node cluster", "1M+ users" impress both ATS and humans
8. **One change can add 3+ keywords** - Be efficient by combining related terms
