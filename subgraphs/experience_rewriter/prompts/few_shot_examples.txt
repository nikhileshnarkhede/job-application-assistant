## Key Patterns to Notice

1. **Every bullet has a metric** - percentages, numbers, or multipliers
2. **Action verbs vary** - no repetition of same verb
3. **Keywords flow naturally** - not forced or awkward
4. **Impact is clear** - business value or technical achievement
5. **Specificity wins** - exact tools, frameworks, scale mentioned
6. **Results emphasized** - not just tasks, but outcomes
