## EMAIL STRUCTURE PRINCIPLES

### Subject Lines
- **Be Specific**: "ML Engineer - 5 Years PyTorch Experience" NOT "Job Inquiry"
- **Include Name/Role**: Makes it easy to search later
- **Create Curiosity**: Without being clickbait
- **Keep Short**: 6-10 words ideal

### Opening Line
- **NO**: "My name is..." or "I am writing to..."
- **YES**: Jump straight to the point or create connection
- Hook them in first sentence

### Body
- **One Paragraph Max** for most emails
- **Two Short Paragraphs** for more complex asks
- **Bullet Points**: Only if listing 3+ specific items
- **White Space**: Break up long sentences

### Closing
- **Clear Ask**: What do you want them to do?
- **Make It Easy**: "Would a 15-minute call work?" not "Let me know"
- **Professional Sign-Off**: Best regards, Thank you, Best

## TONE GUIDELINES

### Professional (Default)
- Respectful but not stiff
- Direct but polite
- Confident but not arrogant

### Friendly
- Slightly warmer
- Can use contractions
- Still professional

### Enthusiastic
- Shows genuine excitement
- Energy without being over-the-top
- Appropriate for startups/creative roles

### Formal
- More traditional language
- No contractions
- Appropriate for senior/executive outreach

## WHAT TO AVOID

1. **Generic Content**: "I am interested in opportunities at your company"
2. **Wall of Text**: No paragraph over 3 sentences
3. **Resume Dump**: Don't list everything, pick ONE highlight
4. **Desperate Tone**: Never apologize for reaching out
5. **Vague Asks**: Always be specific about what you want
6. **Attachments Without Permission**: Don't attach resume cold
7. **Multiple Questions**: One clear ask per email
8. **Self-Focused Language**: Lead with value, not needs
