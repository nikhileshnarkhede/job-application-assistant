# Professional Email Examples - 15 Examples by Type

These examples demonstrate effective email writing for job search.

---

## COLD OUTREACH EXAMPLES (5)

### Example 1: Cold Outreach to Recruiter - Tech
```
SUBJECT: Senior ML Engineer - 6 Years Building Production Systems

Hi Sarah,

I noticed you recruit for Amazon's Applied Science team and recently filled several ML roles. I'm a Senior ML Engineer at Meta with 6 years of experience building recommendation systems serving 500M+ users.

Your team's work on Alexa's NLU caught my attention—I led a similar project that improved intent recognition by 35%. I'd love to learn more about upcoming opportunities on your team.

Would a brief 15-minute call work next week?

Best regards,
Alex Chen
```
**Why It Works:**
- Specific about recipient's work
- One quantified achievement
- Clear, low-pressure ask
- 89 words (perfect length)

## APPLICATION FOLLOW-UP EXAMPLES (3)

### Example 6: Follow-Up - Standard
```
SUBJECT: Following Up: ML Engineer Application - Submitted Jan 15

Hi Recruiting Team,

I submitted my application for the ML Engineer position (Job ID: 12345) on January 15 and wanted to follow up on its status.

I remain very excited about the opportunity to contribute to Stripe's fraud detection systems. My experience building real-time ML pipelines at Square directly aligns with this role.

Please let me know if you need any additional information. I'm available for interviews at your convenience.

Thank you,
Ryan Kim
```
**Why It Works:**
- Specific date and job ID
- Reiterates fit briefly
- Offers flexibility
- 80 words

### Example 8: Follow-Up - After Long Wait
```
SUBJECT: Checking In: Senior Engineer Application (4 Weeks Ago)

I hope this message finds you well. I applied for the Senior Engineer position about four weeks ago and wanted to check if the role is still open.

I understand hiring timelines can shift, but I wanted to reiterate my strong interest. My experience leading distributed systems at Uber makes this role particularly exciting for me.

If the position has been filled, I'd appreciate knowing so I can adjust my search. Either way, thank you for your time.

Chris Park
```

- Not demanding
- Offers graceful exit
- 92 words

## KEY PATTERNS ACROSS ALL EXAMPLES

1. **Subject Lines**: Always specific, include role/context
2. **Length**: 75-110 words for most types
3. **Opening**: Never "I am writing to..."
4. **One Achievement**: Pick ONE relevant highlight
5. **Clear Ask**: Every email ends with specific request
6. **Personalization**: At least 2 specific details
7. **Closing**: Professional but warm
