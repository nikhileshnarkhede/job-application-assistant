# Professional Summary Generation Task

Generate a tailored professional summary for the following job application.

## Target Position
**Company:** {company_name}
**Role:** {role_title}
**Role Type:** {role_type}

## Key Skills to Highlight (from JD)
{matched_skills}

## Candidate Background
**Current/Recent Role:** {recent_role}
**Years of Experience:** {years_experience}

**Top Achievements (with metrics):**
{top_achievements}

**Base Summary (for reference):**
{base_summary}

## Requirements

1. **Length**: 3-4 sentences, 50-80 words
2. **Metrics**: Include at least 1 specific metric from achievements above
3. **Skills**: Naturally incorporate 3-4 matched skills
4. **Tone**: Match the role type:
   - Research/Scientist: Innovation, publications, methodology
   - Engineer: Scale, production, reliability
   - Data Science: Insights, business impact, experimentation

3. **Format**:
   - Start with strong descriptor (Results-driven, Innovative, etc.)
   - No personal pronouns (I, my, me)
   - End with value proposition or company alignment

## Output Format

Return ONLY the professional summary text, nothing else. No quotes, no labels, just the summary.

## Example Output

Results-driven Machine Learning Engineer with 5+ years building production ML systems at scale. Delivered models achieving 34% accuracy improvement and deployed inference pipelines serving 100K+ daily predictions. Expert in PyTorch, deep learning, and AWS infrastructure. Passionate about developing innovative AI solutions that drive measurable business outcomes.
