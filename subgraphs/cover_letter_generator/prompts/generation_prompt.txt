## GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS

Write a complete cover letter following this structure:

### INTRODUCTION (40-60 words)
- Hook: Start with something compelling (company news, shared values, unique qualification)
- Identify the specific position
- Brief statement of why you're interested
- DO NOT start with "I am writing to apply..."

### BODY PARAGRAPH 1 - Skills & Qualifications (60-80 words)
- Highlight 1-2 most relevant achievements with metrics
- Connect directly to job requirements
- Show unique value proposition

### BODY PARAGRAPH 2 - Company Fit (60-80 words)
- Reference specific company research (mission, values, recent news)
- Explain why THIS company specifically
- Show alignment with company goals

### CLOSING (30-50 words)
- Thank the reader
- Express enthusiasm
- Include call to action
- Mention follow-up
