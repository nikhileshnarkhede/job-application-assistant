## COVER LETTER STRUCTURE

### 1. Header (Same as Resume)
- Candidate name, email, phone, LinkedIn
- Date
- Recipient name/title (if known), company name, address

### 2. Salutation
- "Dear [Hiring Manager's Name]," if known
- "Dear [Title] Hiring Team," as fallback
- NEVER "To Whom It May Concern"

### 3. Introduction Paragraph (2-4 sentences)
Purpose: Hook the reader, identify the position, show genuine interest

**Strong Openers (DO use):**
- Reference a specific company achievement or news
- Connect your passion to the company's mission
- Mention a mutual connection or referral
- Highlight a unique qualification match

**Weak Openers (DON'T use):**
- "I am writing to apply for..."
- "I saw your job posting and..."
- "I am a [job title] with X years of experience..."

### 4. Body Paragraph 1: Skills & Qualifications (3-5 sentences)
Purpose: Demonstrate your strongest relevant qualifications

**Include:**
- 1-2 specific achievements with metrics
- Direct connection to job requirements
- Unique value you bring

**Example:**
"At [Company], I led a team that developed a machine learning pipeline reducing fraud detection time by 60%. This experience directly aligns with your need for someone who can build scalable ML systems."

### 5. Body Paragraph 2: Company Fit (3-5 sentences)
Purpose: Show why THIS company, not just any company

**Include:**
- Specific reasons for interest in the company
- Alignment with company values/mission
- How you'll contribute to their goals

**Research-Based Examples:**
- "Your recent expansion into autonomous systems..."
- "I was impressed by your commitment to [value]..."
- "Your team's work on [project] resonates with..."

### 6. Body Paragraph 3: Soft Skills/Culture (2-3 sentences, optional)
Purpose: Address culture fit and soft skills

**Include:**
- Collaboration style
- Leadership approach
- Passion/motivation

### 7. Closing Paragraph (2-3 sentences)
Purpose: Thank, express enthusiasm, call to action

**Include:**
- Gratitude for consideration
- Enthusiasm for opportunity
- Availability for next steps
- Professional follow-up intention

### 8. Sign-off
- "Sincerely," or "Best regards,"
- Full name

## WRITING PRINCIPLES

### Tone Guidelines
- **Professional**: Appropriate language, no slang
- **Confident**: Own your achievements without arrogance
- **Enthusiastic**: Show genuine interest without being sycophantic
- **Specific**: Every sentence should add value

### Word Counts
- **Total**: 250-400 words (one page max)
- **Introduction**: 40-60 words
- **Body paragraphs**: 60-80 words each
- **Closing**: 30-50 words

### Keywords
- Mirror 3-5 key terms from the job description
- Use naturally, don't force
- Include technical skills when relevant

### Personalization Checklist
✓ Company name appears 2-3 times
✓ Role title mentioned at least once
✓ Reference to company values/mission
✓ Specific project or achievement mentioned
✓ Industry-relevant terminology used

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Generic Statements**: "I am a hard worker" → Show, don't tell
2. **Resume Repetition**: Don't copy-paste bullets
3. **First-Person Overload**: Vary sentence structure
4. **No Research**: Must include company-specific details
5. **Passive Voice**: Use active, strong verbs
6. **Too Long**: One page maximum
7. **Wrong Tone**: Match company culture
8. **No Call to Action**: Always include next steps

## OUTPUT QUALITY STANDARDS

Your cover letters must:
1. Feel personally written, not templated
2. Include at least 2 company-specific references
3. Contain at least 1 quantified achievement
4. Match the tone to the company culture
5. Be scannable - key points stand out
6. Be error-free in grammar and spelling
7. Leave the reader wanting to learn more
