# Cover Letter Generation - 10 Examples

These examples demonstrate effective cover letter writing.

## Example 1: Tech Company - Software Engineer

**Context:**
- Company: Google
- Role: Senior Software Engineer
- Company Research: Recent launch of Gemini AI, focus on responsible AI

**Cover Letter:**

```
Sarah Chen
sarah.chen@email.com | (555) 123-4567
San Francisco, CA

January 15, 2024

Google
Mountain View, CA

Dear Google Engineering Hiring Team,

Google's recent launch of Gemini and its commitment to advancing responsible AI represents exactly the kind of innovation I've dedicated my career to enabling. As a software engineer with 6 years of experience building scalable ML infrastructure, I'm excited to apply for the Senior Software Engineer position.

At Meta, I architected a real-time recommendation system processing 50M+ daily requests with 99.99% uptime, reducing latency by 40% while maintaining model accuracy. This experience directly aligns with Google's need for engineers who can build robust, production-grade AI systems at unprecedented scale.

What draws me specifically to Google is your open approach to AI research and the collaborative culture that produced breakthroughs like Transformers. Your team's commitment to making AI beneficial for everyone resonates with my belief that technology should serve humanity. I'm particularly inspired by your AI Principles framework.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience in distributed systems and ML infrastructure can contribute to Google's mission. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Sarah Chen
```

**Why This Works:**
- Opens with specific company news (Gemini launch)
- Quantified achievement (50M+ requests, 99.99% uptime, 40% improvement)
- References company values (responsible AI, AI Principles)
- Shows genuine interest beyond just wanting a job

- Company: Tempus

**Cover Letter:**

```

amanda.wilson@email.com | (555) 456-7890

February 5, 2024

Chicago, IL

Dear Tempus Data Science Team,

Tempus's vision of bringing the power of data to healthcare decisions is deeply personal to me—I lost a family member to cancer that might have been caught earlier with better diagnostic tools. Your work on precision medicine represents the intersection of my technical expertise and my motivation to make healthcare more effective.

As a data scientist with a PhD in Computational Biology, I've developed ML models for cancer prognosis that achieved 92% AUC in predicting treatment response. At Genentech, I built a biomarker discovery pipeline that identified three novel targets now in clinical trials, demonstrating my ability to translate research into real-world medical advances.

What draws me to Tempus specifically is your comprehensive approach—combining genomic sequencing, clinical data, and advanced analytics. Your library of 600,000+ clinical records and partnership with leading oncologists creates opportunities for impact that simply don't exist elsewhere. I want to contribute to your mission of making precision medicine the standard of care.

I would be honored to discuss how my background in computational oncology can advance Tempus's life-saving work. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Dr. Amanda Wilson
```

**Why This Works:**
- Personal connection creates emotional resonance
- Strong technical credentials (PhD, 92% AUC)
- Shows knowledge of company's specific assets (600K records)
- Mission alignment is genuine and specific

## Key Patterns Across Examples

1. **Opening Hook**: Never generic—company news, personal connection, or unique insight
2. **Quantified Achievements**: Every letter has specific numbers
3. **Company Research**: At least 2 specific company references
4. **Role Alignment**: Clear connection between experience and job
5. **Authentic Tone**: Matches company culture naturally
6. **Call to Action**: Every closing includes next steps
7. **Length**: 250-350 words, always one page
